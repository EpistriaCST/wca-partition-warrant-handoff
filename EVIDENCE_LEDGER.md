# Evidence ledger

This ledger separates what has been specified, constructed, observed, attempted, and left open. The labels are epistemic, not project-management labels.

| ID | Claim or task | Evidence in this repository | Status | Defeat condition or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E-01 | A logically prior partition-warrant slot exists when framework observables are invariant across rival partitions. | Formal argument in the primary paper. | **Argued** | A framework-internal observable that independently selects among the relevant rival partitions would remove the underdetermination for that framework. |
| E-02 | `W_CA` is a formally expressible candidate for that slot. | C1–C3, verdict tuple `(tau_0, mu*, kappa)`, and three operational modes. | **Specified** | Internal incoherence, non-operational conditions, or collapse into a criterion already assumed by the target framework. |
| E-03 | `W_CA` can reject passive persistence. | Passively stable negative control in the single-loop witness and reproduced canonical probe suite. | **Constructively supported** | A passively stable, independently non-closure-bearing substrate that satisfies the same operationalization under a non-gerrymandered partition. |
