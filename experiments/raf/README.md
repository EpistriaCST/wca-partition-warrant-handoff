# RAF route

**Status:** protocol draft and unrun code. Not deposited as a preregistration. Not recommended as the principal `W_CA` test.

The route uses reflexively autocatalytic and food-generated sets to provide an organization-fixed partition independently of `W_CA`. That feature initially made RAF networks attractive. It also creates the central inferential weakness: a favorable result may show concordance between a RAF-derived organization and a `W_CA` operationalization without testing whether `W_CA` can independently discriminate a genuine proper sub-closure from a matched, same-dimensional rival.

Further problems are recorded in the handoff:

- the negative carves are not sufficiently justified as serious competitors;
- the harness does not test the modular admit-direction problem;
- C1–C3 are only partially represented by the chemical-dynamical proxies;
- a failure would bear first on this operationalization and substrate class, not automatically on the abstract predicate;
- the protocol's original “pre-registration” language is inapplicable because it was not deposited before execution and was never run.

The materials are preserved because failed routes carry information. A future researcher may redesign this as a limited **RAF–`W_CA` concordance study**, but should not relabel the existing draft as a decisive positive-selection test.
